# Customer Support FAQ

This FAQ covers the questions our support team receives most often. It is used by staff and by the Ops Copilot assistant. AquaPure is a fictional company for this demonstration.

Customers can call 1800-000-000 (toll-free), email support@aquapure.example, or use the chat button in the app. The emergency leak line is available 24 hours a day for active water leakage.

## Why Is My Water Flow Low?

Low flow is usually caused by a clogged sediment or carbon pre-filter, a partially closed inlet valve, low inlet pressure, or a blocked drain line. Check that the inlet valve is fully open, clean or replace the pre-filter, and confirm the drain line is not kinked. If flow does not recover, book a technician visit.

## Why Does the Water Taste or Smell Different?

A new purifier may taste slightly different for the first few days while the carbon fines are flushed out. Run the tap for 10 minutes. If the taste persists, replace the post-carbon filter, clean the tank, and check the UV lamp. If the water smells like chlorine, the carbon filter may be exhausted.

## What Do the Error Codes Mean?

- E1: Low inlet pressure or no water supply. Check the inlet valve, water supply, and pre-filter.
- E2: UV lamp failure or lamp driver fault. Replace the UV lamp; if the error remains, replace the driver.
- E3: Pump overload or high current. Turn off the unit and book a technician; do not keep resetting it.

## My Purifier Is Leaking. What Should I Do?

Close the inlet valve and switch off the power immediately. If water is near electrical fittings, do not touch them. Place a bucket under the leak, remove nearby valuables, and call the emergency leak line. Do not attempt to open the pump or control board yourself.

## How Do I Claim Warranty?

Keep the invoice and serial number ready. Contact support and describe the issue. A certified technician will inspect the product, normally within 48 hours in a service city. Warranty does not cover physical damage or unauthorised repairs. See the warranty policy for full terms.

## How Do I Return a Product?

Request a return within 30 days of delivery. The product must be complete and in original packaging, and consumables must be sealed. Opened products may attract a 10% restocking fee. Filters, membranes, and UV lamps cannot be returned once opened. See the returns policy for details.

## How Often Should I Replace Filters?

Sediment and carbon pre-filters last about 3 months. The RO membrane lasts 6 to 12 months depending on water quality. UV lamps should be replaced every 12 months. The app sends a reminder, and the technician records the next due date.

## What Is the Ideal TDS of Drinking Water?

A TDS of 50 to 300 ppm is generally considered good for drinking. Water above 500 ppm should be treated with RO. TDS is not the only quality indicator; pH, hardness, chlorine, iron, and microbial safety also matter. Test the water at least every six months.

## Can I Install the Purifier Myself?

Self-installation is allowed, but it must follow the installation SOP. Damage caused by incorrect installation is not covered by warranty. In service cities, standard installation is free. Outside service cities, installation can be booked for ₹499 plus travel.

## Does the UV Purifier Remove TDS?

No. A UV purifier disinfects water but does not remove dissolved salts. It is suitable for municipal water with TDS below 500 ppm. Use an RO purifier for higher TDS or high hardness.

## How Do I Reset the Filter Life Indicator?

After replacing a filter, open the settings menu, select Filter Reset, choose the part that was replaced, and confirm. The service app also resets the counter when a technician closes the job. If the indicator does not reset, contact support.

## Can I Transfer the Warranty to a New Owner?

Yes, once. Email the original invoice and proof of sale within 30 days of transfer. The warranty period does not restart.

## What Information Should I Never Share?

Never share your one-time password, card PIN, CVV, or bank password with anyone, including someone claiming to be from AquaPure. Support will never ask for these details. Report suspicious calls to support.
