# Dealer Onboarding SOP

This SOP describes how AquaPure onboards new dealers, sets credit terms, and supports dealer sales. It applies to authorised dealers and distributors in India.

## Required Documents

Before activation, collect and verify:

- GST registration certificate.
- PAN card of the proprietor, partnership, or company.
- Shop and establishment licence or equivalent local trade licence.
- Cancelled cheque and bank account proof in the business name.
- KYC documents for the authorised signatory.
- Signed AquaPure dealer agreement and code of conduct.
- Photographs of the shop, storage area, and signage.

Applications with incomplete documents are kept on hold. Do not activate a dealer or dispatch stock before compliance approval.

## Commercial Terms

The minimum first order is ₹100,000 at dealer price. Standard dealer margin is 18% to 25% depending on category and volume. Credit terms are 15 days from invoice for approved dealers with a clean payment record; new dealers start on prepaid terms for the first three orders. A security deposit may be requested for high-value commercial stock. Freight is paid by the dealer unless the order qualifies for a promotional freight waiver.

## Territory and Pricing

Dealers are assigned a primary territory in the dealer agreement. Dealers must not sell outside their territory without written approval. The maximum retail price is printed on the price list; dealers may discount up to 5% without approval. Online marketplace listings require prior approval and must use approved product content.

## Training and Certification

Every dealer must complete a two-day product and installation training programme before selling purifiers. At least one staff member must pass the installation certification assessment. Certified installers must follow the installation SOP and upload job sheets. Training is refreshed when a new product family is launched.

## Warranty and Service Responsibilities

Dealers must register the product serial number and customer details at the time of sale. Warranty claims are raised through the dealer portal. The dealer is responsible for first-line troubleshooting and for arranging a certified technician. Do not promise warranty coverage that is not in the warranty policy.

## Returns and Dead on Arrival

Dead-on-arrival products must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photographs and a serial number. Approved DOA units are replaced without a restocking fee. Change-of-mind returns are accepted only for unopened stock within 30 days and attract a 10% restocking fee. Consumables are not returnable once opened.

## Support and Escalation

Dealers have a dedicated helpline and email. Routine queries are answered within 4 business hours. Installation or technical escalations follow the escalation and SLA policy. Repeated payment delays, territory violations, or unauthorised online listings may result in suspension.

## Activation Checklist

- Documents verified and dealer agreement signed.
- Dealer code created in the ERP.
- Price list and brand guidelines issued.
- Training completed and installer certified.
- First order paid or credit approved.
- Portal access and support contacts shared.
- Welcome call completed within 2 business days.
