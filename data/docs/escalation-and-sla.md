# Support Escalation and SLA Policy

This policy defines response and resolution targets for customer support tickets. It applies to phone, email, chat, dealer, and app tickets. All times are India Standard Time.

## Priority Definitions

| Priority | Examples | First response | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 Critical | Active leakage near electrical fittings, electrical shock risk, no water to a commercial customer, safety hazard | 2 hours | 24 hours |
| P2 High | Purifier not working, repeated defect, installation delay beyond 48 hours, return pickup failure | 4 business hours | 48 hours |
| P3 Medium | Filter replacement guidance, billing query, delivery delay, warranty documentation | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | General product question, brochure request, feedback | 2 business days | 5 business days |

P1 tickets are worked continuously until contained, including outside normal support hours. P2 and below are handled during support hours, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. The emergency leak line is available 24 hours a day for P1 leakage.

## Ticket Categories

Tickets are categorised as installation, defect, return, filter replacement, billing, or other. The category determines the default priority and routing. A defect involving leakage or electrical risk must be upgraded to P1 even if the customer describes it calmly. An installation ticket becomes P2 when the scheduled visit is more than 48 hours late.

## Escalation Tiers

- L1 Support: first contact, triage, basic troubleshooting, ticket creation, and customer communication.
- L2 Technical Desk: diagnosis, spare-part approval, installation quality, and repeat-defect review.
- L3 Engineering: design faults, safety incidents, firmware or control-board issues, and unresolved repeat failures.
- Support Manager: customer escalation, compensation approval, and SLA breach review.

Escalate to L2 when the first-response target is at risk, when a second visit is needed for the same issue, or when the customer disputes the diagnosis. Escalate to L3 for safety incidents, three failures of the same component, or a defect pattern across multiple units. Escalate to the manager when a P1 is not contained within 8 hours or a customer requests compensation.

## SLA Breach Handling

When a target is missed, update the ticket with the reason, the next action, and a revised commitment. Notify the customer before the target expires, not after. P1 breaches are reviewed daily. Repeated P2 breaches trigger a root-cause review. Do not close a ticket without customer confirmation or three documented contact attempts.

## After-Hours Coverage

Only P1 leakage and electrical-safety tickets are handled after hours. The on-call technician acknowledges the ticket within 2 hours, provides telephone containment advice, and schedules an on-site visit for the next available slot. Non-P1 tickets received after hours are queued for the next business day.

## Closure and Feedback

Resolve the issue, record the root cause and parts used, and ask the customer for a CSAT rating. Close the ticket only after the customer confirms the fix or after three documented attempts. Reopen a closed ticket if the same issue recurs within 7 days.
