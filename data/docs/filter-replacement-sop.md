# Filter and Consumable Replacement SOP

Filters protect the purifier and determine water quality. Replace consumables on schedule even if the water still looks clear. Use only genuine AquaPure parts so that the warranty remains valid.

## Replacement Schedule

| Consumable | Typical replacement interval |
| --- | --- |
| Sediment filter (5 micron) | Every 3 months or 3,000 litres |
| Carbon block pre-filter | Every 3 months or 3,000 litres |
| Post-carbon polisher | Every 6 months |
| RO membrane 80/100 GPD | Every 6 to 12 months, depending on TDS and usage |
| UV lamp | Every 12 months or 9,000 hours |
| Mineral, alkaline, or copper cartridge | Every 6 months |
| Whole-house 10-inch pre-filter | Every 3 months |
| Descaling cartridge | Every 6 months |
| Commercial membrane 4040 | Every 6 months or when rejection falls below 85% |

The service app records the installation date and calculates the next due date. Customers with high TDS, high hardness, or heavy usage may need earlier replacement.

## Signs That a Filter Needs Attention

- Reduced flow at the tap or longer tank-fill time.
- Unusual taste, odour, or cloudiness.
- Outlet TDS rising above 15% of inlet TDS, or RO rejection falling below 85%.
- UV lamp indicator flashing or an E2 error on the display.
- Visible sediment or discolouration in the pre-filter housing.
- Water leaking from a filter housing.

Do not wait for a complete failure. A clogged pre-filter puts extra load on the pump and membrane.

## Replacement Procedure

1. Shut off the inlet valve and turn off the purifier.
2. Open the tap to release pressure, then close it.
3. Place a towel and a shallow tray under the filter housing.
4. Unscrew the housing using the supplied wrench; do not force a cracked housing.
5. Remove the old cartridge and clean the housing with clean water. Do not use detergent.
6. Check the O-ring; replace it if cut, stretched, or hardened.
7. Insert the new cartridge, confirm the orientation, and lubricate the O-ring with a thin film of food-grade silicone grease.
8. Hand-tighten the housing, then tighten a quarter turn with the wrench.
9. Open the inlet valve slowly and check for leaks.
10. Flush the system for 10 minutes before use.
11. Reset the filter life counter in the service app.
12. Record the part numbers and outlet TDS on the job sheet.

## Sanitisation and Hygiene

Wear clean gloves. Do not touch the membrane surface. Discard used cartridges in accordance with local waste rules; do not reuse a cartridge by washing it. If the tank has not been cleaned for six months, clean and sanitise it during the same visit.

## Genuine Parts and Warranty

Non-genuine cartridges may fit physically but can damage the membrane or pump. Damage caused by non-genuine parts is excluded from warranty. Keep the part invoice attached to the service record.

## Customer Communication

Explain why the replacement is due, show the old cartridge, and quote the price before starting work. If the customer declines a required replacement, record the refusal, explain the risk, and ask them to sign the job sheet. Do not leave a leaking housing or an unflushed system in service.
