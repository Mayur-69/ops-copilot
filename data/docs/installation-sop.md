# Purifier Installation Standard Operating Procedure

This SOP is for AquaPure-certified technicians installing residential RO, UV, gravity, and whole-house purifiers. Follow every step and record the results in the service app before closing the ticket.

## Scheduling Commitment

Installation is scheduled within 48 hours of order confirmation in service cities. Outside service cities, installation is scheduled within 3 to 5 business days. The customer may reschedule once without charge if the request is made at least 24 hours before the appointment. A second reschedule may attract a visit fee.

## Pre-Installation Checks

Before drilling or connecting anything, confirm:

- The model and serial number match the invoice and the customer's order.
- The inlet water supply is available and the shut-off valve works.
- Inlet pressure is between 0.3 and 3 bar for residential purifiers and between 1 and 4 bar for whole-house systems.
- The power point is 230 V AC, properly earthed, and within reach of the adapter cable.
- There is enough wall space and load-bearing support for the mounting bracket.
- The customer understands where the drain line will run and approves the route.
- A TDS test is recorded at the inlet and, after flushing, at the outlet.

If inlet pressure exceeds 3 bar, install an approved pressure-reducing valve before commissioning. If TDS exceeds 2,000 ppm, advise the customer that a commercial system or additional pre-treatment may be required.

## Installation Steps

1. Shut off the water supply and open a downstream tap to release pressure.
2. Mark and drill the mounting holes; fit wall plugs and the bracket.
3. Mount the purifier level and confirm it is stable.
4. Connect the inlet hose to the cold-water line through the supplied diverter valve.
5. Route the drain line with a continuous fall and secure it to prevent backflow.
6. Connect the product-water tap and tighten all fittings by hand plus a quarter turn.
7. Connect the power adapter to the earthed socket; do not use extension boards.
8. Open the inlet valve slowly and check every joint for leaks.
9. Flush the system for 10 to 15 minutes with the tank drain open, then close the drain.
10. Let the tank fill, test outlet TDS, and confirm the auto-off function works.
11. Check for leaks again under pressure and wipe all connections dry.
12. Demonstrate filter replacement, tank cleaning, and the error display to the customer.
13. Activate the warranty in the service app and upload before/after photographs.
14. Ask the customer to sign the digital job sheet and provide feedback.

## Safety Requirements

- Turn off electrical power before touching the adapter, pump, or control board.
- Wear gloves and safety glasses when drilling or handling filter housings.
- Never install a purifier near a heat source, open flame, or in direct sunlight.
- Ensure the socket is earthed. If earthing is doubtful, stop and advise the customer to consult an electrician.
- Do not use non-AquaPure spare parts during installation.
- Keep the work area dry and clean; clean up all drilling dust and water.

## Post-Installation Handover

Explain the filter replacement schedule, how to shut off the inlet valve in an emergency, and how to read error codes. Confirm that the customer has the invoice, warranty card, and support number. Record the serial number and installation date in the service app. If the customer reports a leak or low flow during handover, fix it before leaving.

## Escalation

Escalate to the technical desk if inlet pressure cannot be brought within range, if the wall cannot support the unit, if the electrical point is unsafe, or if the product is damaged on arrival. Do not leave an unsafe installation in service.
