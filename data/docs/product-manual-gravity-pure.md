# AquaPure Gravity 20L User Manual

Model: AP-GR-20. The AquaPure Gravity 20L is a non-electric gravity-fed purifier for households that want simple, low-maintenance water filtration without a power connection.

## Product Overview

The Gravity 20L uses gravity to pass water through a four-stage filter candle system. It does not need electricity, a pump, or plumbing. It reduces turbidity, chlorine, odour, and some dissolved impurities, but it does not remove dissolved salts or guarantee microbiological safety. For water with high TDS or suspected biological contamination, use an RO purifier or boil the water.

## Purification Stages

1. Sediment layer traps sand, silt, and visible particles.
2. Activated carbon removes chlorine, colour, and odour.
3. Silver-impregnated carbon inhibits bacterial growth in the candle.
4. Fine ceramic barrier provides final particle removal.

## Suitable Water Sources

The Gravity 20L is suitable for clear municipal water with low turbidity. It is not suitable for water with TDS above 500 ppm, visible colour, oily films, or known microbial contamination. It does not remove fluoride, arsenic, nitrate, or lead. If the source is unsafe, boil the water after filtration or use a certified RO system.

## Assembly and First Use

Wash all removable parts with clean water before first use. Soak the filter candle in clean water for 15 minutes, then fit it into the upper chamber. Fill the upper chamber with water and let it filter. Discard the first two batches. Do not use detergent on the candle.

## Daily Use

Fill the upper chamber with water and cover it. Water filters into the lower chamber by gravity. Use the tap to dispense water. Do not fill above the maximum mark. Empty and clean the lower chamber if water is left unused for more than two days.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

Clean the upper and lower chambers every 2 weeks with clean water and a soft brush. Do not use soap or abrasive cleaners. Scrub the ceramic candle gently every month; do not use sandpaper. Replace the filter candle every 12 months, or earlier if the flow becomes very slow or the taste changes. Replace the carbon layer according to the printed schedule.

## Troubleshooting

**Very slow flow:** The candle may be clogged. Remove it, scrub it gently, and rinse. If flow does not recover, replace the candle.

**Water tastes unpleasant:** Clean the chambers, replace the candle, and check the source water.

**Visible particles in filtered water:** The candle may be cracked or improperly seated. Replace or reseat it.

**Water has a green tint:** Clean the lower chamber and keep it dry when not in use. Do not store water for long periods.

## Safety and Warranty

Use only clear water within the stated limits. The Gravity 20L has a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Consumable candles are excluded. See the warranty policy for full terms.
