# AquaPure RO 500 User Manual

Model: AP-RO-500. This manual covers installation, operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting for the AquaPure RO 500 residential water purifier.

## Product Overview

The AquaPure RO 500 is a seven-stage reverse osmosis purifier designed for residential use. It combines sediment filtration, carbon filtration, reverse osmosis, post-carbon polishing, UV disinfection, and mineral enhancement. The purifier stores treated water in an 8-litre food-grade tank and dispenses through a separate chrome faucet.

## Purification Stages

1. Sediment filter (5 micron) removes sand, silt, and suspended particles.
2. Carbon block filter removes chlorine, odour, and organic compounds.
3. RO membrane (80 GPD) removes dissolved salts, heavy metals, and most contaminants.
4. Post-carbon polisher improves taste and removes residual odour.
5. UV lamp disinfects the treated water.
6. Mineral cartridge adds beneficial minerals for taste.
7. Ultrafiltration membrane provides a final barrier against particles.

## Operating Instructions

1. Confirm that the inlet valve is open and the power adapter is connected to an earthed socket.
2. Turn on the purifier using the touch button. The tank fills automatically and the unit switches off when full.
3. Discard the first tank of water after installation or after replacing the membrane.
4. Open the faucet to dispense water. The tank refills automatically.
5. Close the inlet valve if the purifier will not be used for more than a week.

Do not run the purifier with the inlet valve closed. The pump may run dry and be damaged.

## Display and Error Codes

The display shows tank level and filter status. Error codes are:

- E1: Low inlet pressure or no water. Check the supply and pre-filter.
- E2: UV lamp failure. Replace the lamp or driver.
- E3: Pump overload. Switch off and book service.

## Maintenance Schedule

Replace the sediment and carbon pre-filters every 3 months. Replace the post-carbon filter and mineral cartridge every 6 months. Replace the RO membrane every 6 to 12 months, or when rejection falls below 85%. Replace the UV lamp every 12 months. Clean the tank every 6 months.

## Troubleshooting

**No water from the faucet:** Check the power supply, inlet valve, and tank level. If the display shows E1, restore inlet pressure.

**Low flow:** Replace a clogged pre-filter, check for a kinked tube, and confirm inlet pressure is at least 0.3 bar.

**Water tastes flat:** Replace the mineral cartridge and post-carbon filter.

**Leak from the housing:** Close the inlet valve, switch off power, and book service. Do not overtighten a cracked housing.

**Noisy operation:** A slight pump hum is normal. Loud knocking may indicate air in the system or a failing pump; book service.

## Safety

Use a properly earthed 230 V socket. Do not install near heat or direct sunlight. Do not use extension boards. Keep the drain line secure and free of kinks. This appliance is not intended for use by children without supervision.
