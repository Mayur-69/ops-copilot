# AquaPure UV 300 User Manual

Model: AP-UV-300. The AquaPure UV 300 is a non-RO ultraviolet purifier for municipal water that is already low in dissolved salts.

## Product Overview

The UV 300 uses a 6-stage treatment train ending in an 11 W ultraviolet lamp. It removes suspended particles, chlorine, odour, and microorganisms but does not remove dissolved salts. It is intended for water with TDS below 500 ppm and is not a substitute for RO where TDS or hardness is high.

## Purification Stages

1. Sediment filter removes particles.
2. Carbon block removes chlorine and odour.
3. Activated carbon polisher improves taste.
4. UV lamp inactivates bacteria and viruses.
5. Post-carbon filter removes residual taste.
6. Final sediment barrier protects the outlet.

## Suitable Water Sources

The UV 300 is suitable for treated municipal water with TDS below 500 ppm, low hardness, and no visible colour or sediment. It is not suitable for borewell water with high TDS, iron, or hardness. For such water, use an RO purifier or additional pre-treatment. UV treatment does not remove lead, arsenic, fluoride, nitrate, or dissolved salts.

## Operation

Open the inlet valve and connect the adapter to an earthed socket. The UV lamp runs while the unit is powered. Dispense water from the faucet; the tank refills automatically. Run the tap for 10 minutes after installation or lamp replacement. Do not stare directly at the UV lamp; the chamber is shielded, but do not open it while powered.

## Maintenance

Replace the sediment and carbon pre-filters every 3 months. Replace the post-carbon filter every 6 months. Replace the UV lamp every 12 months or after 9,000 hours, even if it still glows, because the disinfection output declines with age. Clean the tank every 6 months.

## Troubleshooting

**UV lamp not glowing:** Check the power supply and lamp connection. If the lamp is dark, replace it. If the new lamp does not light, the driver may have failed.

**Low flow:** Replace the pre-filter and check the inlet pressure.

**Water has an odour:** Replace the carbon filters and clean the tank.

**TDS is high:** UV does not reduce TDS. Test the source water and consider an RO system if TDS is above 500 ppm.

## Safety and Warranty

Use an earthed socket and keep the unit away from water splashes at the electrical connections. The UV 300 has a 24-month warranty against manufacturing defects; consumables and UV lamps are excluded. See the warranty policy for details.
