# AquaPure Whole-House 2000/3000 User Manual

Models: AP-WH-2000 and AP-WH-3000. These whole-house systems treat water at the main inlet so that every tap in the home receives filtered water.

## Product Overview

The Whole-House 2000 and 3000 are multi-stage inlet treatment systems with a backwashable pre-filter, carbon block filtration, and UV disinfection. They are designed for independent houses, villas, and small apartment buildings. They do not remove TDS; use a point-of-use RO purifier for drinking water where TDS is high.

## Installation Requirements

Installation must be performed by a certified plumber or AquaPure technician. The system requires a bypass valve, a drain connection for backwash, an earthed power point for the UV lamp, and a protected location away from sunlight and freezing. Inlet pressure above 4 bar requires a pressure-reducing valve. The system must not be installed on a line that is used for hot water.

## Operation

Open the inlet valve slowly and check for leaks. The pre-filter and carbon stages operate without power; the UV lamp requires power. The system does not need a storage tank. Backwash the pre-filter weekly by turning the valve to the backwash position for 2 to 3 minutes, then rinse for 1 minute and return to service. Record the backwash date.

## Maintenance

Replace the 10-inch pre-filter every 3 months. Replace the carbon block every 6 months. Replace the UV lamp every 12 months. Inspect the housings and O-rings every 6 months. Clean the UV sleeve with a soft cloth if it becomes cloudy. Replace the whole-house pre-filter immediately after any muddy-water event.

## Troubleshooting

**Low pressure at taps:** Backwash the pre-filter, check the inlet pressure, and replace a clogged cartridge.

**UV alarm:** Replace the UV lamp; if the alarm persists, check the lamp driver.

**Leak at a housing:** Close the inlet valve, depressurise, and inspect the O-ring. Replace a damaged O-ring; do not overtighten.

**Water bypassing filtration:** Check the bypass valve position and confirm the cartridges are seated correctly.

## Safety and Warranty

Do not open a pressurised housing. Close the inlet valve and release pressure first. The whole-house system carries a 36-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Consumables, UV lamps, and damage from freezing or water hammer are excluded. See the warranty policy for details.
