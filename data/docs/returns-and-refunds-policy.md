# Returns and Refunds Policy

AquaPure wants customers to be confident when buying a water purifier. This policy explains when a product can be returned, how refunds are processed, and which items are excluded. The policy applies to purchases made through the AquaPure website, app, dealer network, and authorised marketplaces.

## Return Window

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. The delivery date is the date recorded by the courier or the installation completion date, whichever is later. Business-to-business orders have a 15-day return window from delivery.

Returns requested after the applicable window are not accepted unless the product is covered by a warranty claim.

## Conditions for Return

To be accepted, a return must meet all of the following conditions:

- The product is in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals, and mounting hardware.
- The product has not been damaged by the customer, including during installation.
- Filters, membranes, and UV lamps are unused and still sealed.
- The invoice or order number is provided.
- The product was purchased directly from AquaPure or an authorised seller.

Products that fail these conditions may be refused or may receive a partial refund after inspection.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned once opened or used:

- Filter cartridges, RO membranes, UV lamps, and other consumables.
- Custom-built or made-to-order commercial systems.
- Clearance and final-sale items marked as non-returnable at checkout.
- Installation, installation-related services, and extended warranty plans.
- Products purchased from unauthorised resellers.

## Restocking Fee

An unopened product returned within the window has no restocking fee. An opened product that is complete, undamaged, and resalable is subject to a 10% restocking fee based on the product price before tax. A defective product reported within 48 hours of delivery has no restocking fee.

## Refund Timeline

After the returned product is picked up and inspected, AquaPure processes the refund within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. For cash-on-delivery orders, the refund is made by bank transfer within 7 to 10 business days after the customer submits bank details. Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to a defect or an AquaPure error.

## Return Pickup

Pickup is scheduled within 2 to 4 business days in service cities and within 5 to 7 business days elsewhere. The customer must keep the product packed and available at the pickup address. If pickup fails because the customer is unavailable, a second attempt is made; a third attempt may attract a pickup fee.

## Damaged or Defective on Arrival

Report transit damage or a dead-on-arrival defect within 48 hours of delivery. Include photographs of the packaging, product, and serial label. AquaPure will arrange a replacement or a full refund, including shipping charges, with no restocking fee. Do not install a product that arrives damaged.

## Order Cancellation

An order can be cancelled free of charge before it is dispatched. Once dispatched, cancellation is treated as a return and the return conditions apply. Refunds for cancelled prepaid orders are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

## Exchanges

Customers may exchange a product for a different model within 30 days of delivery. The replacement must be of equal or higher value; the customer pays the difference. Any promotional discount on the original order is recalculated when an exchange is processed.

## How to Start a Return

Contact support with the order number and reason for return. Support will confirm eligibility, create a return ticket, and schedule pickup. Keep the return ticket number for follow-up. Refunds are not issued until the warehouse inspection is complete.
