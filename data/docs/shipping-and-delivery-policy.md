# Shipping and Delivery Policy

AquaPure ships water purifiers, filters, spare parts, and accessories across India. This document describes dispatch times, delivery estimates, charges, and what to do when a shipment is delayed or damaged.

## Serviceable Pincodes

AquaPure delivers to more than 18,000 pincodes. The checkout page confirms serviceability before payment. Some remote pincodes are served only by prepaid courier and may not support cash on delivery.

## Dispatch Times

In-stock orders are dispatched within 24 business hours. Orders that include a backordered item are dispatched within 3 to 5 business days after the item is restocked. Dispatch is not attempted on Sundays or national holidays. Customers receive an SMS and email with the courier name and tracking number when the shipment leaves the warehouse.

## Delivery Estimates

| Destination | Estimated delivery after dispatch |
| --- | --- |
| Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune | 3 to 5 business days |
| Other state capitals and tier-2 cities | 5 to 7 business days |
| Remote and north-east pincodes | 7 to 10 business days |

These are estimates, not guarantees. Festive periods, heavy rain, strikes, and courier disruptions can add 2 to 4 business days. AquaPure does not deliver on Sundays.

## Shipping Charges

Standard shipping is free for orders with a product subtotal of ₹15,000 or more. Orders below ₹15,000 are charged ₹99. Whole-house systems and commercial RO systems carry a fixed shipping charge of ₹499. Remote pincodes may attract an additional ₹299 remote-area charge, which is shown at checkout.

## Installation and Delivery

Standard installation is included free of charge for purifier orders in service cities. Customers outside service cities can request installation for ₹499 plus travel charges, or choose self-installation with telephone guidance. Installation is scheduled separately from delivery and normally happens within 48 hours of delivery in service cities.

## Damaged Shipments

Inspect the outer packaging before signing. If the carton is visibly damaged, note the damage on the courier's proof of delivery and photograph the carton. Report any transit damage to AquaPure within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the packaging, product, and serial label. AquaPure arranges a replacement or refund for verified transit damage. Damage reported after 48 hours may be treated as customer-caused damage.

## Failed Delivery Attempts

The courier makes up to three delivery attempts on consecutive business days. If all attempts fail, the shipment returns to the warehouse. AquaPure contacts the customer to arrange re-shipment, which may attract a re-shipping fee equal to the original shipping charge.

## Tracking and Support

Tracking is available from the order confirmation page and the link in the dispatch SMS. For delivery exceptions, contact support with the order number. Do not share one-time passwords or payment details with couriers.

## Large and Commercial Orders

Whole-house and commercial systems are shipped by freight partner. The customer must provide a receiving contact, unloading assistance, and a safe storage area. Freight deliveries are scheduled by phone at least 24 hours in advance. Installation of commercial systems is quoted separately.
