# AquaPure Warranty Policy

This policy applies to products sold in India by AquaPure Water Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (AquaPure), a fictional company used for this demonstration. The warranty period starts on the invoice date, or on the installation date when installation is performed by an AquaPure-certified technician and that date is later.

## Warranty Periods at a Glance

| Product family | Standard warranty |
| --- | --- |
| Residential RO, UV, alkaline, copper, and smart purifiers | 24 months |
| Gravity purifiers | 12 months |
| Whole-house systems | 36 months |
| Commercial RO systems | 12 months |
| Spare parts and accessories | 12 months |
| UV lamps | 6 months |
| Filter cartridges and membranes | 3 to 6 months, as printed on the part |

Consumable items are covered only for manufacturing defects present at the time of delivery. Normal wear, clogging, and reduction in flow caused by use are not manufacturing defects.

## What Is Covered

The standard warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures that occur during normal residential or commercial use, including:

- Leaking filter housings or moulded parts that were not damaged during installation.
- Failure of the booster pump, solenoid valve, SMPS adapter, or control board under normal electrical supply.
- Electrical or mechanical failure of the UV lamp holder or lamp driver.
- Structural cracks in the storage tank that are not caused by impact.
- Failure of the whole-house control valve or UV chamber under normal inlet pressure.

AquaPure will repair or replace the defective part at its discretion. Replacement parts carry the balance of the original warranty or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Physical damage, cracks, dents, or broken parts caused by dropping, impact, or improper handling.
- Damage caused by water pressure above 3 bar for residential purifiers or above 4 bar for whole-house systems, unless an approved pressure-reducing valve was installed.
- Damage caused by incorrect voltage, power surges, lightning, or use of a non-approved adapter.
- Repairs, modifications, or installations performed by anyone other than an AquaPure-certified technician.
- Consumable filters, membranes, and UV lamps after the printed replacement interval, or damage caused by not replacing them on schedule.
- Water quality outside the inlet limits stated in the product manual, including excessive hardness, iron, or suspended solids.
- Commercial use of a product marketed for residential use.
- Cosmetic wear, scratches, or normal discolouration.
- Damage caused by fire, flood, earthquake, or other force majeure events.

## How to Make a Claim

1. Contact AquaPure support with the invoice number, product serial number, and a short description of the problem.
2. Support creates a ticket and may ask for photographs or a short video of the issue.
3. A certified technician inspects the product, normally within 48 hours in a service city and within 5 working days elsewhere.
4. If the fault is covered, AquaPure repairs or replaces the part at no charge for parts or labour.
5. If the fault is not covered, the technician provides a written estimate before any chargeable work begins.

Do not attempt to repair the product yourself. Unauthorised repairs void the remaining warranty.

## Extended Warranty

Residential customers can extend the standard warranty to a total of 36 months by purchasing an extended warranty within 30 days of the invoice date. The extended warranty costs 8% of the product's printed maximum retail price for each additional year. Extended warranty is available only for residential RO, UV, alkaline, copper, and smart purifiers. It is not available for gravity purifiers, consumables, or commercial systems.

## Transfer of Warranty

The warranty may be transferred once to a new owner when the product is sold with the original invoice. The new owner must email proof of sale and the new address within 30 days of the transfer. The warranty period does not restart.

## Replacement Policy

If the same covered defect is repaired three times within the warranty period, AquaPure will replace the product with the same model or a comparable model. Replacement requires all service reports and the original invoice. A replacement does not extend the warranty period.

## Service Cities

On-site inspection is available in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Jaipur, Kochi, Indore, and Chandigarh. Customers outside these cities may be asked to courier the product to the nearest service centre; AquaPure covers one-way courier charges for approved warranty claims.
